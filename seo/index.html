FAQ

Insurance BPO questions, answered

What should I look for in an insurance BPO provider?

Look for licensed agents in the lines you write, compliance QA on every interaction rather than a sample, proven surge capacity for claims peaks, and references from insurers in your market. Ask for published outcomes, not just headcount. Foundever clients see 10–30% lower cost to serve across claims and servicing and a +9 point NPS uplift.

Can you scale claims support after a major storm or CAT event?

Yes. We stand up specialist claims capacity in 2–3 weeks for peak events and CAT season, drawing on 10,000+ insurance associates worldwide. FNOL simulation training and accuracy-focused agent assist keep quality high while claim volumes spike.

Do you provide claims outsourcing for UK and US insurers?

Yes. Foundever supports insurers in both the UK and the US with claims handling, policy servicing, complaints and retention. Every program runs on audit ready compliance: full interaction recording, embedded QA frameworks and regulatory reporting.
